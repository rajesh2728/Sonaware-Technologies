Lifecycle Roadmap

Our Cloud Implementation Journey

A proven, structured path to migrate and operate your tenant with zero business disruption.

1. Discovery & Readiness Audit

Evaluating existing email systems, local storage shares, user licenses, domain health, and network bandwidth.

2. Tenant & Domain Architecture

Setting up custom domain records, DNS verification, SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication, and administrative role baselines.

3. Staged / Cutover Migration

Migrating mailboxes, contact lists, calendar events, and shared directories with minimal-to-zero downtime.

4. Intune & Endpoint Configuration

Enrolling enterprise devices, rolling out baseline corporate configurations, and pushing business applications automatically.

5. Purview & Security Hardening

Configuring Defender policies, DLP rules, anti-phishing parameters, and Conditional Access compliance guardrails.

6. Training & Continuous Admin

Providing administrative knowledge transfer, user onboarding manuals, and SLA-backed ongoing managed helpdesk support.

Cloud Service Scope Clarification
