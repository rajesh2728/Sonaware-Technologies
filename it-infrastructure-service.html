Delivery Process

Our Infrastructure Implementation Lifecycle

A methodical rollout roadmap designed to modernize your technology without business interruption.

1. Site & Hardware Audit

Evaluating existing switches, routers, servers, cabling routes, UPS capacities, and hardware life cycles.

2. Architecture & Sizing Plan

Designing optimized network topology diagrams, compute specifications, storage tiers, and growth projections.

3. Procurement & Provisioning

Guiding hardware procurement, mounting rack equipment, configuring patch panels, and establishing power redundancies.

4. OS & Role Configuration

Installing operating systems, setting up domain services, file shares, print servers, and local access rights.

5. Security & Baseline Hardening

Disabling unneeded ports, locking down administrative credentials, establishing firewall boundaries, and enabling telemetry.

6. Handover & SLA Support

Comprehensive infrastructure documentation, runbooks, and ongoing preventative support contracts (AMC).

ℹ️

Infrastructure Scope Clarification

Our IT infrastructure service provides design, hardware configuration, operating system setup, and ongoing administrative maintenance. Physical equipment (servers, switches, access points, cabling materials) is procured directly by the client or billed separately through authorized enterprise hardware distributors.
