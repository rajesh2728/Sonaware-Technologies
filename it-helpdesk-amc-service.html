Service Delivery

How We Manage Your IT Ecosystem

A dependable, tiered support workflow built to resolve employee issues rapidly.

01 / Instant Access

Tiered Remote Desktop Troubleshooting

Most user issues can be diagnosed and fixed without waiting for a physical visit. Our technical team connects securely to get your staff working right away.

  • Direct desktop control via encrypted support tools
  • Quick fix for Outlook profile crashes, printer spoolers, and driver errors
  • Operating system boot optimizations and antivirus definitions updates
02 / Scheduled Health

Periodic Maintenance & Physical Servicing

Our engineers perform structured quarterly or monthly health checks to catch degrading hard drives, failing power supplies, and thermal bottlenecks.

  • Physical blowing, blower cleanup, and thermal paste reapplication
  • UPS battery load testing and emergency backup verification
  • Network cable testing, RJ45 re-crimping, and switch port audit
03 / Longevity & Strategy

Hardware Lifespan Extension & Upgrades

Save substantial capital expenditures by strategically upgrading older legacy workstations with high-speed SSDs and RAM rather than complete machine replacements.

  • Mechanical HDD to NVMe/SATA SSD migration with zero data loss
  • RAM capacity expansion for multitasking workflows
  • End-of-life hardware recommendations and transparent buyback advice

AMC Contract Scope Clarification

Our standard Annual Maintenance Contracts cover unlimited remote technical support, preventative servicing schedules, and labor for on-site troubleshooting. Replacement hardware parts (such as new motherboards, batteries, screens, or SSDs) are provided under OEM warranty or quoted at transparent commercial component rates in non-comprehensive agreements.
